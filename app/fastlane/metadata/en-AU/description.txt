YesYes is a dating app for adults looking for casual encounters – who want them agreed and safe.

VERIFIED PEOPLE
Everyone verifies with an ID and a selfie. They are who they say they are, and a ban applies to the person, not just the account. We don't keep your ID.

BLIND AGREEMENT
Each of you marks what you're into on your own: yes, maybe or no. You both only see what you agree on – nobody sees the other's "no". You also agree on the place, condoms, staying sober and a safe word upfront. Consent only counts here and now, and you can say no to anything once you're there.

SAFETY MODE
• Someone you trust gets a link to your location and the status of your date.
• PIN check-ins – if you don't check in, our server sends a text even if your phone is dead.
• SOS by holding a button.
• A duress PIN that looks exactly like your normal one but quietly raises the alarm.
• A fake call as an excuse to leave.

PRIVACY
Discreet icon, notifications without content, face or fingerprint lock, photos without location. Messages are encrypted and the agreement's details are deleted after the date.

AFTER THE DATE
A private question about how it went – the other person never sees your answer. Cross a boundary and you're out.

18+ only.
