# Mainstream dating apps: relationship-intention and lifestyle labels, what users pick, and what the labels do

Method note (2026-10-25): Fetching pages directly failed because DNS lookups were blocked in this environment (techcrunch.com, help.hinge.co and filecache.mediaroom.com all returned ENOTFOUND). The findings below therefore come from search-result excerpts of the cited pages, not full-page reads. Most option lists date from launch coverage in 2022–2024. Apps may have renamed options since then, so check them in the live apps before copying wording.

## Q1. Tinder: Relationship Goals, Relationship Type, Interests, and how many users choose "short-term fun"

### Takeaway
Tinder has six Relationship Goals (launched Dec 2022) and five Relationship Types (launched Mar 2023). Interests ("Passions") are capped at 3–5. Among members who set a goal, about 40% want long-term and about 13% want "short-term fun", which is one of the least chosen options worldwide.

### Cited Findings
- **Relationship Goals (launched 14 Dec 2022)** has six options shown as a banner on the profile: "Long-term partner", "Long-term, but open to short", "Short-term, but open to long", "Short-term fun", "New friends", "Still figuring it out". — [TechCrunch, 2022-12-14](https://techcrunch.com/2022/12/14/tinder-launches-relationship-goals-feature)
- In testing, "more than 50% of users" added a Relationship Goal to their profile (company claim). — [TechCrunch, 2022-12-14](https://techcrunch.com/2022/12/14/tinder-launches-relationship-goals-feature)
- Tinder's 2023 Future of Dating report says that among members who use the feature, 40% are looking for a long-term relationship, while "Short term fun" is "among the lowest selected Relationship Goal on Tinder globally" at 13%. — [Tinder UK Future of Dating Report 2023 (PDF)](https://filecache.mediaroom.com/mr5mr_tinder_uk/179315/Tinder%20FOD%20Report%202023%20FINAL%20%281%29.pdf); the same 40% vs 13% figures appear in Tinder's statement to [NBC News (2023)](https://www.nbcnews.com/pop-culture/lifestyle/many-online-daters-arent-seeking-actual-dates-rcna94163)
- A Tinder Gen Z brand campaign gave 31% of Gen Z members looking for long-term and 14% looking for something short-term. The article is undated and the figure approximate. — [Mediaweek AU](https://mediaweek.com.au/tinder-announces-new-brand-campaign-that-celebrates-gen-z)
- An academic-style study of 1,387 users aged 18–74, covered by NBC News in 2023, reported that 65.3% of Tinder users surveyed were already in a relationship and that many were not seeking actual dates. Tinder disputed the study. — [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/pop-culture/lifestyle/many-online-daters-arent-seeking-actual-dates-rcna94163)
- Tinder's 2022 Year in Swipe found that "1 in 10" respondents aged 18–24 (UK, US, Australia) prefer "situationships" as a lower-pressure way to develop a relationship. — [YPulse, 2022-12-02](https://www.ypulse.com/newsfeed/2022/12/02/tinders-year-in-swipe-is-here-and-situationships-are-2022s-top-trend/)
- **Relationship Type (launched March 2023, alongside pronouns)** offers: Monogamy, Ethical non-monogamy, Open relationship, Polyamory, Open to exploring. It sits on the profile and was borrowed from Hinge. — [TechCrunch, 2023-03-16](https://techcrunch.com/2023/03/16/tinder-will-now-let-daters-define-their-relationship-type-add-pronouns-to-their-profile); [PinkNews, 2023-03-17](https://www.thepinknews.com/2023/03/17/tinder-polyamory-open-relationships-pronouns)
- Rationale: in Tinder's survey of 4,000 people aged 18–25 (US, UK, AU, CA), 51% want monogamy and 41% are open to or seeking non-monogamy. Among those, open relationships (36%) and hierarchical polyamory (26%) were the most popular. — [PinkNews, 2023](https://www.thepinknews.com/2023/03/17/tinder-polyamory-open-relationships-pronouns); [Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/news/tinder-launches-relationship-types-and-pronoun-options)
- Initial rollout markets for Relationship Type: US, Brazil, France, Netherlands, India, Taiwan, Italy, Spain, Thailand, Sweden, Germany, Mexico, Canada. — [Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/news/tinder-launches-relationship-types-and-pronoun-options)
- **Passions/Interests (launched 2020)**: members pick a minimum of 3 and a maximum of 5. They show on the profile and highlight what you have in common with a match. — [Social Samosa, 2020](https://www.socialsamosa.com/2020/09/tinder-new-feature-passions/); [Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/news/more-tinder-users-can-display-interests-with-expansion-of-passions/)

### Inferences
- Tinder splits labels into three axes: goal or time horizon (single choice), relationship structure (single choice), and interests (3–5 picks). This is a good template for keeping "what I'm looking for" separate from "how I do relationships" and "what I like".
- The hybrid options ("long-term, open to short" and "short-term, open to long") let people hedge. A casual app could mirror this with a softer hedge such as "casual, open to more".
- Even on Tinder, which is seen as a hookup app, only about 13% of goal-setters publicly pick "short-term fun". That suggests stigma or self-presentation pressure around stating casual intent openly, which points toward softer wording ("fun, no pressure") or private matching signals.

### Gaps
- No Tinder breakdown was found for the percentages choosing "New friends", "Still figuring it out" or the hybrid options, nor for Relationship Type choices. No 2024–2026 Year in Swipe data on goals was found.
- No Tinder data was found on whether showing a goal changes match rate, ghosting or reports.

## Q2. Hinge, Bumble, OkCupid, Badoo, Hily: option lists and recent changes

### Takeaway
Most mainstream apps offer one single-choice "intention" scale running from life partner to short-term, plus "figuring it out". The exceptions: Bumble allows up to two intentions and has explicit casual labels ("Fun, casual dates", "Intimacy without commitment"); OkCupid has historically offered "Hookups"; Badoo makes a three-option intention mandatory.

### Cited Findings
- **Hinge Dating Intentions (launched June 2022):** "Life partner", "Long-term relationship", "Long-term relationship, open to short", "Short-term relationship, open to long", "Short-term relationship", "Figuring out my dating goals", plus "Prefer not to say". There is also an optional free-text "Backstory". — [TechCrunch, 2022-06-23](https://techcrunch.com/2022/06/23/hinges-feature-easier-for-users-be-upfront-about-their-dating-intentions/embed/); [Good Men Project](https://goodmenproject.com/featured-content/hinge-finally-released-this-new-feature/)
- "Life partner" is defined as dating to find someone to share life with, including marriage, kids and finances. — [The Brag](https://thebrag.com/hinge/)
- **Hinge Relationship Type (launched Nov 2022):** "Monogamy", "Non-monogamy", "Figuring out my relationship type". It is optional, shown in the "Virtues" section, and has an optional backstory text. Hinge said 15% of its Gen Z and LGBTQIA+ users were exploring their relationship type or intentionally seeking non-monogamy. — [TechCrunch, 2022-11-16](https://techcrunch.com/2022/11/16/hinges-new-feature-is-a-small-win-for-users-in-non-monogamy-relationships/embed/); [Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/news/hinge-launches-relationship-type-feature/); [Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/wellness/hinge-relationship-type-feature-non-monogamy)
- Hinge profiles require three prompts. In April 2026 Hinge added an optional "Date Ideas" section where users highlight three preferred first-date activities to signal intent to actually meet. — [Hinge newsroom](https://hinge.co/newsroom/date-ideas-feature)
- **Bumble Dating Intentions badges (2024 relaunch, CEO Lidiane Jones):** options include "Life partner", "Fun, casual dates", "Intimacy without commitment" and "Ethical non-monogamy". Users can show up to two intentions on their profile, and shared interests are surfaced at the top of profiles. — [Irish News](https://www.irishnews.com/news/uk/dating-app-bumble-unveils-new-features-to-boost-womens-safety-VDUQC5K64FMTNEHHE54XP3VELM/); [Impact](https://www.impactonnet.com/more-from-impact/bumble-unveils-new-features-and-interest-badges-for-better-connections-9270.html); [KRDO/CNN](https://krdo.com/?p=1263498)
- Filtering by dating intention is a paid Advanced Filter on Bumble Premium. The help center describes the "Looking for" badge as a way to signal "something casual or serious". — [Bumble help: badges](https://bumble.com/help/什么-是-bumble-徽章); [Bumble Buzz: filters](https://bumble.com/the-buzz/how-to-use-filters-on-bumble)
- In April–May 2024 Bumble introduced "Opening Moves" (pre-written prompts) and dropped the requirement that women message first. — [BusinessWire, 2024-04-30](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240430323928/en/Bumble-Gives-Women-More-Choice-to-Make-the-First-Move)
- **Bumble 2025–2026 (Whitney Wolfe Herd's return):** a "women-first" reset. In March 2026 Bumble announced "Bee", an AI assistant that learns values, goals, lifestyle and dating intentions to find mutual compatibility, and "chapter-based" profiles expected in spring 2026. Rollout status is unconfirmed. FY2025 revenue fell about 10% to $966M and paying users fell 11.5%. — [Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/news/bumble-unveils-ai-assistant-bee-and-chapter-based-profiles/); [Fortune, 2026-03-12](https://www.fortune.com/2026/03/12/bumble-2025-earnings-stock-increase-whitney-wolfe-herd-comeback)
- **OkCupid:** historically "Looking for" offered Hookups, New friends, Short-term, Long-term, with a separate monogamous vs non-monogamous setting. These are older sources, and current labels were not confirmed. — [Smart Customer Q&A](https://www.smartcustomer.com/faq/okcupid.com/does-men-listed-in-this-site-actually-look-for-casual-dating-or-long-term-dating-i-have-been-cough-in-3-different-situations-where-these-people-are-avoiding-meeting-in-person-advise-1). Earlier versions had a "casual sex" checkbox. — [Society Pages, 2013](https://thesocietypages.org/cyborgology/?p=14000)
- OkCupid added "open relationship" and "non-monogamous" options in 2014, plus partner-linking so that monogamy-seekers do not see linked couples. — [CNN, 2016-01-08](https://amp.cnn.com/cnn/2016/01/08/living/okcupid-polyamorous-open-relationships-feat)
- OkCupid reported a 45% increase in profile mentions of non-monogamy terms nationally between 2021 and 2023. — [Axios Seattle, 2024-04-11](https://www.axios.com/local/seattle/2024/04/11/polyamory-nonmonogamy-seattle-washington)
- **Badoo:** a mandatory intention with three options, "Here to date", "Ready for a relationship", "Open to chat". Features stay limited until one is set. Badoo's own research claims 78% of daters feel stress or burnout from mismatched intentions (company figure). — [Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/news/badoo-launches-new-feature-to-get-singles-to-state-their-dating-intentions/)

### Inferences
- Bumble is the only mainstream app found with explicitly sexual-but-softened casual wording ("Intimacy without commitment") and multi-select (up to 2). Both are useful precedents for a casual app.
- Badoo shows a mandatory, very short (3-option) intention can be enforced at onboarding.
- Hinge's free-text "Backstory" and "Date Ideas" show a pattern: a structured label plus optional context.

### Gaps
- No info was found on Hily's intention options.
- Current (2025–2026) OkCupid "Looking for" wording, and whether "Hookups" still exists, is unconfirmed.
- Badoo's launch date could not be verified.
- Hinge and Bumble usage shares per option were not found.

## Q3. Research and surveys: intention transparency, mismatched expectations, ghosting and harassment, women's safety, and number of tags

### Takeaway
Industry surveys consistently report frustration with mismatched intentions and ghosting. No rigorous independent study was found showing that intention labels reduce ghosting or harassment; claims of benefit are company rationale. Evidence on women's safety strongly supports meeting in public first. Choice-overload evidence (6 vs 24 profiles) concerns partner options, not tag counts.

### Cited Findings
- Coffee Meets Bagel's 2025 US survey (n>1,000, ages 21–35): 61% seek a spouse, 31% a long-term partner, and 93% find dating difficult (industry survey). — [Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/featured/most-u-s-singles-still-aim-for-long-term-relationships)
- CMB's 2026 Australian report: ghosting is the top app frustration (41%). — [CMB blog](https://coffeemeetsbagel.com/blog/dating-realness-report-aussie-singles-are-prioritizing-falling-in-love-over-finances-health-and-career-in-2026)
- The app "After" (Austin, Sep 2024) requires users to give a reason for ending a conversation. Options include "we have different goals", which signals that goal mismatch is a recognised reason chats end. — [Stylus](https://stylus.com/consumer-attitudes/dating-app-after-tackles-ghosting-romance-burnout)
- Hinge framed Relationship Type as a way to "minimize unsuited matches" (company rationale, not measured). — [Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/news/hinge-launches-relationship-type-feature/)
- Australian Institute of Criminology (2023): 72% of surveyed dating-app users had experienced some form of sexual harassment, aggression or violence from someone they connected with via an app. — reported via [Besedo](https://besedo.com/blog/can-dating-apps-keep-women-safe/) (secondary; original report not opened)
- Pew (reported Feb 2023): significantly more men than women use apps to seek casual sex. Coverage links this partly to the harassment women face. — via [Besedo](https://besedo.com/blog/can-dating-apps-keep-women-safe/) (secondary)
- 97% of respondents take first-date precautions such as meeting in public and sharing location (DatingNews survey, industry). — via [Besedo](https://besedo.com/blog/can-dating-apps-keep-women-safe/)
- Spain (Young Women's Federation, 2023, n=900 Tinder users): 3 in 4 women felt pressured to have sex on dates after meeting online; about 20% reported violent sexual assault (headline). — [Olive Press, 2023-03-15](https://theolivepress.es/spain-news/2023/03/15/study-finds-20-of-female-dating-app-users-have-experienced-violent-sexual-assault)
- A 91% figure for women wanting better safety features exists (survey headline; not opened). — [Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/news/91-of-women-want-better-dating-app-safety-features-survey-finds/)
- Aljasim & Zytko (2023, ACM, participatory design with women) propose features such as a "guardian" that monitors safety during in-person meetings. — [ACM DOI 10.1145/3567559](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1145%2F3567559)
- Choice overload: D'Angelo & Toma (Media Psychology, 2016–17, n=152 undergraduates). People choosing from 24 profiles were less satisfied a week later than those choosing from 6, and more likely to switch. Sources conflict on whether reversibility worsened satisfaction. — [UW–Madison news](https://news.wisc.edu/online-dating-study-shows-too-many-choices-can-lead-to-dissatisfaction); conflicting interpretation in [Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/news/does-the-abundance-of-choice-online-dating-offers-actually-impair-singles-satisfaction/) and [Psychology Today](https://www.psychologytoday.com/gb/blog/love-digitally/201805/do-you-find-online-dating-confusing-this-may-be-why)

### Inferences
- Because women are more exposed to harassment and less likely to state casual intent, a casual app's tag list could include safety and consent-oriented "how" tags (e.g. "meet in public first", "chat before meeting", "take it slow") alongside "what" tags. This is an inference; no source tested such tags.
- The choice-overload finding concerns partner profiles. Applying it to tag counts is analogy, not evidence.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed 2020–2026 study was found that measures the causal effect of intention labels on match rate, ghosting or harassment.
- The originals of the Pew and AIC reports were not opened, and their numbers are secondhand.
- No study was found on the optimal number of profile tags.

## Q4. UX patterns for tag taxonomies (categories, max selections, public vs private, filters)

### Takeaway
The industry pattern is a few separate axes: one intention (single choice, or up to 2 on Bumble), one relationship structure, and 3–5 interests. Labels are public on the profile, and filtering by them is often a paid feature. No formal UX research specific to dating tags was found.

### Cited Findings
- Tinder: 6-option single-choice goal, 5-option structure, interests min 3 / max 5. — [TechCrunch 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/12/14/tinder-launches-relationship-goals-feature); [TechCrunch 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/03/16/tinder-will-now-let-daters-define-their-relationship-type-add-pronouns-to-their-profile); [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/2020/09/tinder-new-feature-passions/)
- Hinge: single-choice intention with a "Prefer not to say" escape, optional structure shown in Virtues, optional free text, 3 required prompts, and an optional 3-item Date Ideas section. — [TechCrunch 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/06/23/hinges-feature-easier-for-users-be-upfront-about-their-dating-intentions/embed/); [Hinge newsroom](https://hinge.co/newsroom/date-ideas-feature)
- Bumble: up to 2 intentions; filtering by intention is behind Premium Advanced Filters; filtering by interest badges is offered. — [Impact](https://www.impactonnet.com/more-from-impact/bumble-unveils-new-features-and-interest-badges-for-better-connections-9270.html); [Bumble filters](https://bumble.com/the-buzz/how-to-use-filters-on-bumble); [Bumble Buzz: interest filter](https://bumble.com/the-buzz/filter-by-interest-badges?amp=1)
- Badoo: mandatory 3-option intention. — [Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/news/badoo-launches-new-feature-to-get-singles-to-state-their-dating-intentions/)
- OkCupid: hides linked non-monogamous couples from people seeking only monogamy, an example of a label acting as a hard filter. — [CNN 2016](https://amp.cnn.com/cnn/2016/01/08/living/okcupid-polyamorous-open-relationships-feat)

### Inferences
- A defensible design based on these precedents would be one primary "looking for" choice (or up to 2), short lists of about 3–7 options per axis, an "open/figuring it out" escape, 3–5 lifestyle or interest tags, and optional free text. Sensitive tags (kinks, ENM details) could be private match-only signals rather than public badges, given the low public uptake of "short-term fun" (13%) and the harassment data.
- Hard filters on intention (as OkCupid does for monogamy) reduce mismatches but shrink the pool. Bumble monetises them.

### Gaps
- No A/B or academic data was found on the effect of public vs private tags, or of max-selection limits, on dating outcomes.
- No CHI/CSCW paper was found specifically on dating-profile tag taxonomies within this search budget.
