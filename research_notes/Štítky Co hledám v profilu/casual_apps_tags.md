## Feeld: Desires, Interests, connection/relationship types, how many, visibility

### Takeaway
Feeld separates **Desires** (a fixed, curated, filterable list of 30+ tags in about 7 categories, shown on profile with shared ones highlighted) from **Interests** (free text, not filterable). It also has separate **Relationship type** (structure), **Sexuality** and **Gender** fields (20+ options each), plus profile linking with up to 5 partners. Third-party guides say the selection cap is 10 Desires; official sources quoted here do not mention a cap.

### Cited Findings
- Feeld official: "over 30 desires that are common among Feeld members"; members cannot create custom desires; definitions live in a glossary — [Feeld Support: Desires, Relationship Types, Sexualities, and Genders Explained](https://support.feeld.co/hc/en-gb/articles/18822038569884-Desires-Relationship-Types-Sexualities-and-Genders-on-Feeld-Profiles-Explained)
- Feeld's own guide groups desires into about **seven categories**: Kink & fantasy (e.g. bondage, role play, BDSM), Group dynamics (e.g. threeways, with FFM named), Casual encounters (e.g. flirting, exploration, dates), One-on-one, Open-to-exploring dynamics (ENM, open relationships, poly setups, searching for couples), Friendship, Emotional connection — [Feeld: What are Interests and Desires on Feeld?](https://feeld.co/ask-feeld/how-to/what-are-interests-and-desires-on-feeld); [Feeld blog version](https://feeld.co/blog/feeld-guides/what-are-interests-and-desires-on-feeld)
- Desires can be used as **search filters**. **Interests are free text** ("type anything you want") and are **not** used for filtering — [Feeld: Interests and Desires](https://feeld.co/ask-feeld/how-to/what-are-interests-and-desires-on-feeld)
- When you view another profile, the desires you **share** with that member are shown. Holding down a term opens its glossary definition — [Feeld: Interests and Desires](https://feeld.co/ask-feeld/how-to/what-are-interests-and-desires-on-feeld); [Feeld Glossary](https://feeld.co/glossary?show=Desires)
- Edit path: Settings → Edit profile → Desires; can be changed at any time — [Feeld Support: Edit your profile details](https://support.feeld.co/hc/en-gb/articles/9406784112028-Edit-your-profile-details)
- Change: **"Celibate"** was added as a selectable desire — [Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/news/feeld-adds-celibacy-as-new-desire-option/)
- The glossary defines group configurations, e.g. **FFF** = threesome where all participants identify as women; also BDSM and Dominant — [Feeld Glossary](https://feeld.co/glossary)
- Third-party item lists (approximate, not official): FFF, FFM, MMF, MMM, GGG, BDSM, Casual, Kink, Group, Poly, Threeway, Watching (voyeur). Says users can pick **up to 10 Desires** — [dude-hack.com Feeld Desires 2025](https://dude-hack.com/what-are-the-desires-on-feeld/)
- A 2026 third-party guide groups desires as relationship dynamics (casual, dating, long-term, polyamory, monogamy, open relationship), experience types (threesome, group play), power dynamics, and connection styles — [Poise: Feeld Desires List (2026)](https://trypoise.app/feeld-guide/feeld-desires-explained)
- The App Store editorial story says "more than 25" desire options, while the support site says "over 30", so the count has grown or differs by source — [App Store story: Everything you need to know about Feeld](https://apps.apple.com/de/iphone/story/id1758768292?l=en-GB); [Feeld Support](https://support.feeld.co/hc/en-gb/articles/18822038569884-Desires-Relationship-Types-Sexualities-and-Genders-on-Feeld-Profiles-Explained)
- Profiles also list relationship structure (open, poly, partnered-and-curious, solo), with orientation and gender from 20+ options each — [nss magazine 2026](https://www.nssmag.com/en/lifestyle/43809/what-is-feeld-dating-app-sex-positivity)
- Profile linking (couples/polycules): up to **five** linked partners as of 2025, per a third-party review — [Datezie Feeld review 2026](https://www.datezie.com/feeld-review/)

### Inferences
- Feeld's taxonomy has three layers: **structure** (relationship type), **desires** (curated, filterable, public), and **interests** (free text, public, not filterable). Group configurations are spelled out as gender-letter codes (FFM/MMF/etc.).
- Desires appear to be public on the profile, with shared ones emphasised. No source mentions private or hidden desires.

### Gaps
- Could not get the full verbatim official list of 30+ Desires (glossary page not fetchable). The 10-desire cap comes only from a third-party source.
- There is no official "Looking for / connection type" field separate from Desires. Older Feeld versions had "Looking for: Men/Women/Couples…", but I could not confirm the current state.
- I did not find an official changelog of when categories were introduced.

## Pure: statement model, time limits, tags

### Takeaway
Pure is built around an **ad** you must publish before you can see others. Ads are short-lived (reported as 1 hour) and chats expire after 24 hours unless both agree to remove the timer. The tag system is **Turn-ons**, which are public, mix sexual and non-sexual items, and can be filtered on. I found no evidence of a fixed "tonight" option label.

### Cited Findings
- You must publish your own ad to see others' ads. The ad shows city, gender, sexuality, age, height and the **number of Turn-ons** — [Pure FAQ](https://pure.app/content/en/faq)
- Chats expire after 24 hours. Both parties can agree to turn off the timer. Screenshots are blocked and trigger a warning to both — [Pure FAQ](https://pure.app/content/en/faq)
- Ads reportedly disappear after **one hour** (secondary source) — [manuals.dev: Pure's One-Hour Posts](https://manuals.dev/technology/pures-one-hour-posts-replace-the-swipe-and-wait-routine); review sites also claim profiles vanish after 24 h — [DatingScout Pure review](https://www.datingscout.com/pure/review)
- Turn-ons: tap to add or remove. **Selected turn-ons are visible to everyone who views your profile** — [Pure FAQ](https://pure.app/content/en/faq)
- Turn-ons include sexual items (kinks, interaction types) and non-sexual ones such as Feminism, Literature, Artsy Dates, Been to Therapy — [DatingScout](https://www.datingscout.com/pure/review); "dozens of options, whether it is intelligence or BDSM practices" — [Pure Journal 2022](https://pure.app/content/en/journal/sum-dating-2022); press examples: kissing, cosplay, cuddles, sapiosexuality — [BRICKS Magazine 2024](https://bricksmagazine.co.uk/2024/03/14/meet-pure-the-sex-positive-dating-app-that-lets-women-set-their-own-rules/)
- A "Perfect Match" toggle in the Turn-ons filter shows only ads matching **all** selected turn-ons — [Pure FAQ](https://pure.app/content/en/faq)
- Positioning shift: the current App Store title is "PURE: Dating with Intention" / "Open-Minded Dating App", rather than pure hookup — [App Store CA](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/pure-dating-with-intention/id690661663)

### Inferences
- Pure expresses intent mainly through **time-limitation** (ephemeral ad, timed chat) plus turn-on tags rather than a "looking for" picker. The urgency ("now/tonight") is built into the mechanic, not a tag.

### Gaps
- No verbatim list of Turn-ons and no limit on the count was found. The 1-hour vs 24-hour ad lifetime is reported inconsistently.

## Grindr: Looking For, Meet At, Right Now, Tribes, NSFW, sexual health

### Takeaway
Grindr's official multi-select **"I'm Looking For"** has 6 options: Chat, Dates, Friends, Networking, Relationship, Hookups. **"Meet At"** has 5: My Place, Your Place, Bar, Coffee Shop, Restaurant. Both sit in the Preferences section of Edit Profile. **Right Now** is a separate, time-limited (1 hour) real-time feed, rolled out globally in 2025. Tribes: "My Tribes" plus "Tribes I'm Into" (max 3). The health fields are optional and public once filled in, but not filterable.

### Cited Findings
- "I'm Looking For": multi-select **Chat, Dates, Friends, Networking, Relationship, Hookups**. "Meet At": **My Place, Your Place, Bar, Coffee Shop, Restaurant**, often used to signal hosting. Both are in Edit Profile → Preferences — [Grindr Help: How to Build Your Profile](https://help.grindr.com/hc/en-us/articles/4402336949523-How-to-Build-Your-Profile)
- The Australian eSafety guide describes the looking-for range as "relationship" to "right now". This likely mixes in the Right Now feature, and the official list doesn't include it — [eSafety Commissioner: Grindr](https://esafety.gov.au/key-topics/esafety-guide/grindr)
- Tribes: "My Tribes" (e.g. Bear, Daddy, Discreet, Rugged, Twink, Geek) and **"Tribes I'm Into", up to 3** — [Grindr Help: How to Build Your Profile](https://help.grindr.com/hc/en-us/articles/4402336949523-How-to-Build-Your-Profile)
- Sexual health: HIV Status, Last Tested Date, Testing Reminders (opt-in, default Off), Sexual Health FAQ — [Grindr Help](https://help.grindr.com/hc/en-us/articles/4402336949523-How-to-Build-Your-Profile); Sexual Health Practices options: condoms, DoxyPEP, PrEP, HIV undetectable, prefer to discuss. Vaccinations: COVID-19, mpox, meningitis — [Grindr blog: World Sexual Health Day](https://www.grindr.com/blog/grindr-celebrates-world-sexual-health-day-with-profile-health-fields-and-filters)
- Health section is optional and visible to anyone viewing the profile once published. Users **cannot search/filter** by vaccination, HIV status or last tested date. Grindr says it does not share self-reported health info with advertisers (responding to a 2018 controversy) — [Grindr Help: Health information privacy](https://help.grindr.com/hc/en-us/articles/8792479838483-Health-information-privacy); [BuzzFeed News 2018](https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/azeenghorayshi/grindr-hiv-status-privacy)
- Right Now (help center): a session lasts **1 hour** and a purple "droplets" emoji shows on the profile while active. There is an optional status text. Free allotment is **2 sessions/week**, resetting Friday 5:00 PM local time. Explicit photo uploads are available in AU, CA, NZ, UK, US. "Discreet Posts" are not linked to the profile — [Grindr Help: Right Now](https://help.grindr.com/hc/en-us/articles/52846752470803-Right-Now)
- Right Now timeline: trials in Australia and Washington D.C. → 15 new cities in March 2025 (NYC, London, Paris, São Paulo…), described as a real-time feed separate from the grid with text and photos shown for one hour, and "10 free sessions per week" at that time — [Nasdaq/BusinessWire 2025-03-25](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/looking-grindrs-right-now-feature-expands-15-new-cities-2025-03-25); global rollout announced May 29, 2025 — [Grindr blog: Right Now launches globally](https://www.grindr.com/blog/ready-sweat-go-right-now-launches-globally); Q4 2025: live in 200+ cities, with a Maps view in 15+ cities — [Grindr Q4 2025 shareholder letter](https://s203.q4cdn.com/415221501/files/doc_financials/2025/q4/GRND_SHL_Q42025.pdf)
- **Conflict:** the free allotment is 10/week in the March 2025 press release vs 2/week on the current help page, which suggests the free quota was reduced later — sources above.

### Inferences
- Grindr splits intent into three channels: a persistent multi-select **Looking For** (low-intensity, public), a logistics field **Meet At** (hosting), and an ephemeral, high-intensity **Right Now** mode (1 hour, separate feed). This is a clear model for separating "general intention" from "immediate availability".

### Gaps
- "Accept NSFW Pics" field: none of the search extracts mentioned it, so I could not verify its current options. From memory (unverified), the options were roughly "Never / Not At First / Yes Please".
- Position/role and body-type fields were not verified in this pass.

## Taimi, Lex, 3Fun, Kasual, AdultFriendFinder: intent/kink/role tags (category level)

### Takeaway
These apps range from **almost no tags** (3Fun older versions, Kasual, Lex text-first) to **very rich kink taxonomies** (AFF). Taimi combines a relationship-goal field (friendship / conversation / date) with "vibe" tags that include relationship style (monogamy/polyamory) and power dynamics (dom/sub).

### Cited Findings
- **Taimi:** users choose whether they're looking for friendship, conversation or a date — [DatingAdvice Taimi review](https://www.datingadvice.com/advice/taimi-review); "vibe" options describe interests and intent, including monogamy/polyamory and domination/submission — [Enby Meaning Taimi review 2026](https://www.enbymeaning.com/read/taimi-review); sexuality options: Gay, Lesbian, Bisexual, Pansexual, Queer, Asexual, Demisexual, Straight — [Wikipedia: Taimi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taimi)
- **Lex:** text-first personals (title + short written post), user-added **tags**, one optional photo. Posts can also be about events or friendships — [Wikipedia: Lex (app)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lex_(app)); [TheDatingGal Lex review](https://thedatinggal.com/reviews/lex)
- **3Fun (threesome/swinger app):** sources conflict. One review says there are no desire or interest tags, only a free-text "Bio". A more recent (2026) review describes searchable, grouped interests with **up to 10** selectable, plus ten identity options (single/couple types) — [VanillaSwingers 3Fun vs Feeld](https://www.vanillaswingers.com/3fun-vs-feeld-a-couples-no-bs-3fun-review/); [MarriageScience 3Fun review 2026](https://marriagescience.com/best-dating-sites/3fun/)
- **Kasual:** anonymity-first. Photos are optional (avatars common). Only gender, orientation and location are required. I found no clear "looking for" tag selector. DatingScout shows lifestyle tags such as "Into music" and "Vegetarian/vegan" — [Kasual review](https://www.kasualapp.com/kasual-review/); [DatingScout Kasual](https://www.datingscout.com/kasual/review)
- **AdultFriendFinder:** at signup you choose gender (man, woman, couple, trans), orientation, and "Looking For" (love, casual, friends, hanging out). The target-gender selector is Men / Women / Couples / Trans. There is an extensive kink-tag list ("from bondage to shaving"). Users mark kinks as **enjoy vs curious**, can search by kinks/interests, and can join interest groups — [DatingNews AFF review](https://www.datingnews.com/dating-sites/adult-friend-finder/); [Vice AFF review](https://www.vice.com/en/via/adultfriendfinder-review/); [DatingSitesReviews AFF](https://www.datingsitesreviews.com/staticpages/index.php?page=adult-friendfinder)

### Inferences
- AFF's "enjoy / curious" two-level marking is a notable pattern (graded interest instead of binary), useful for a sex-positive taxonomy.
- Group/couple apps (3Fun, Feeld, AFF) treat **who you are** (single, couple, MF/FF/MM couple) as a primary field, separate from what you're looking for.

### Gaps
- No official help-center documentation was found for Taimi, Lex, 3Fun, Kasual or AFF option lists. All the details above come from reviews. Exact verbatim labels are unverified.

## Hily, Badoo, Lovoo, Czech apps: intention options

### Takeaway
Mainstream apps use a **single-choice intention** with 3–6 options. Badoo has exactly 3 (mandatory, shown on profile, used as a match filter). Hily offers about 5 goals (friendship, casual dating, chat partner, long-term, flirting). Lovoo offers Friendship / Flirt / Relationship. Czech classified-style sites use categories like vážný vztah / flirt / nezávazně / dopisování / přátelství. Lidé.cz has shut down.

### Cited Findings
- **Badoo:** the Intentions filter requires choosing **one of three**: "Here to date", "Ready for a relationship", "Open to chat". Matching is only with people who chose the same, and it can be changed at any time — [Badoo blog](https://badoo.com/en/the-blog/dating-tips/how-to-keep-the-chat-alive-during-summer-holiday-season); intention became **mandatory**, other features are limited until it is set, and it is **shown on the profile** — [Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/news/badoo-launches-new-feature-to-get-singles-to-state-their-dating-intentions/)
- **Hily:** "Are you interested in a friendship, casual dating, a partner to chat with, a long-term relationship, or just flirting?". Profiles show badges where goals align. Filtering by relationship type is premium — [App Store: Hily](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hily-dating-app-meet-date/id1250946975); [Photofeeler Hily review](https://blog.photofeeler.com/hily/); a third-party review lists additional goals (sugar dating, BDSM/fetish, swinging/open, "exploring my options"), which is unverified — [DatingScout Hily](https://www.datingscout.com/hily/review)
- **Lovoo:** markets "Freundschaft, Flirt oder Beziehung" coexisting. At signup there are 5 questions: name, birthday, gender, whom you seek, and what you seek. Filters are limited to age and distance — [Lovoo DE](https://lovoode.com/); [top6partnersuche Lovoo](https://www.top6partnersuche.de/datingseiten/lovoo/); [singleboersencheck Lovoo 2026](https://www.singleboersencheck.de/test/lovoo/). Unlike Tinder, Lovoo lets you state whether you're after just sex or a steady relationship — [andreas-lorenz.com Lovoo vs Tinder](https://www.andreas-lorenz.com/online-dating-tipps/lovoo-oder-tinder-was-ist-besser/)
- **Lidé.cz (Seznam):** the dating service was **definitively shut down** and all data deleted — [Novinky.cz](https://www.novinky.cz/clanek/internet-a-pc-seznamka-lide-cz-definitivne-skoncila-40345217)
- **Elite Date (CZ):** an agency-style service for **vážný vztah** (serious relationship), targeting ages 25–45 — [arecenze.cz Elite Date](https://www.arecenze.cz/online-seznamky/elite-date/)
- **Seznamka.cz:** categories run from vážný vztah through flirt to shared interests (sport, travel). There is a "Nezávazně" section, and historically about 60% of ads were in "nezávazný flirt" (older article) — [Seznamka.cz](https://www.seznamka.cz/); [Seznamka.cz Nezávazně](https://www.seznamka.cz/nezavazne/); [E15 Miluj mě mobilně](https://www.e15.cz/magazin/miluj-me-mobilne-1229888)
- **Seznamit.cz:** "dating_types" filter values include Seznámení, vážné, do postele, flirt, dopisování, přátelství — [Seznamit.cz flirt](https://www.seznamit.cz/hledat/muzi/flirt)

### Inferences
- Czech-language precedents for labels: "vážný vztah", "nezávazně / nezávazný flirt", "flirt", "přátelství", "dopisování", "do postele". These are useful as localisation references.
- Mainstream apps use single-select and make the choice mandatory (Badoo). Hookup apps use multi-select (Grindr) or many tags (Feeld/Pure/AFF).

### Gaps
- Lovoo's exact current verbatim labels, and whether the choice is single- or multi-select, are unconfirmed.
- Elitedate.cz's exact form fields are unconfirmed, and it is uncertain whether "Elite Date" in the review is the same service.
- I found no current dedicated Czech hookup app with a documented tag taxonomy.
